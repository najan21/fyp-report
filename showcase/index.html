Lighter. Tougher.
Built to scale.

Electric scramblers win on instant torque and mechanical simplicity, but battery mass and the shock of that torque push the frame harder than a petrol bike ever does.

This project adapts an existing 400 cc frame to carry a battery box, vehicle control unit and 25 kW motor, and to survive landing, bump and cornering loads, at a target of 120 kg, SGD 3,500 and 10,000 units a year.

3.1 · FRONT FRAME

Where the load paths begin

The front frame carries the steering head, upper structure and battery cradle. It is the main target for weight reduction and for packaging the new battery and motor mounts, while the forks and wheels stay as they are.

3.3 · BATTERY

Battery

A 72 V, 60 Ah lithium-ion pack gives 4.32 kWh, about 37 Wh per kilogram of bike. The new battery bay has to protect the cells from vibration, impact and debris while staying simple enough for series production.

3.4 · MOTOR

YM PMSM mid motor

An air-cooled, IP67-sealed permanent magnet motor with an integrated reduction gearbox. Mid-mounting keeps unsprung mass low and puts the weight central in the frame, and the final drive can be retuned by swapping sprockets.
