01 · Introduction

Electric torque is instant. The frame has to keep up.

Electric scramblers win on throttle response, torque and mechanical simplicity, but battery mass and the shock of instantaneous torque push the frame harder than a petrol bike ever does. This project adapts an existing 400 cc scrambler frame to house a battery box, VCU and 25 kW motor, and to survive landing, bump and cornering loads.

TARGET 01

Cut the weight

Bring the complete bike, tyres included, to 120 kg so it competes with existing electric off-road bikes.

TARGET 02

Package the powertrain

Fit the battery, VCU and mid-mounted motor inside the donor frame, protected from vibration, impact and debris.

TARGET 03

Build it at volume

Choose materials and joining methods that hit SGD 3,500 retail at 10,000 units a year.
